## SUMMARY

All negative moments, by severity:

(MAJOR) The river all-in verdict said "✅ Good — betting for value with ~92%" and "You win ~92% · need ~0%", then I immediately LOST my whole 200BB stack to a flush on an obvious three-heart board. The verdict is technically defensible (decision-vs-range, ~92% vs random), but nothing on the result screen reconciles "92%" with "Result: you lost $200" — no "great decision, unlucky result / that's variance" bridge. A trusting newcomer reads 92%, loses everything, and feels the app contradicted itself. The reconciling language ("these are long-run averages, not this one hand") DOES exist — but it's hidden inside a collapsed "Show the numbers" expander.

(MAJOR) Unit ($/BB) inconsistency throughout the feedback panel. With the toggle in BB mode (stack/pot/buttons/header all show BB, and even the live Mental Math says "Call 60 BB into 302 BB"), the Hand Review list and the end-of-hand "Result" line still print DOLLARS — e.g. "you called $2 · pot $3", "Result: you lost $200" — shown simultaneously with the BB "out of chips" dialog. Mixed units on one screen will confuse a newcomer who switched to BB.

(MINOR) The "Result: ... no money won/lost this hand. For a deeper write-up run /poker-coach last" line is shown MID-HAND, while I'm still deciding the flop/turn/river. A final "Result" + end-of-hand recap appearing before the hand is over made me think the hand had already ended.

(MINOR) The persistent panel text "The numbers below (Mental Math) are for this flop/turn decision" promises numbers that are NOT visible until I both act AND expand the collapsed Mental Math section. Before acting (and on preflop spots) the promised mental-math numbers are absent, and Mental Math shows "Deal a hand and reach the flop to use Mental Math."

(MINOR) In instant-feedback-OFF mode, the panel says the review comes "when the hand ENDS", but the per-decision Hand Review list actually populates LIVE after each action. Only the big top verdict/equity block is hidden. The "when the hand ends" copy is misleading.

(MINOR) Layout collisions at constrained window sizes. At 800x600 the top seat (Bot 3) is clipped off the top edge and the center "Pot" readout is overlapped by the "You" hero seat box. At 600x900 the center "THIS ROUND" action summary is partially hidden behind the "You" seat box. The table keeps its fixed desktop oval and does not reflow for narrow/short-height widths. (Action buttons themselves were never clipped at any size; 1280x520 and 1366x768 were clean.)

(MINOR) The coaching-depth choice (Conceptual / Equity+Heuristics / Strict charts) had little visible effect on preflop verdicts — every preflop verdict was tagged "chart-based" and cited "the baseline chart", even though I selected "Equity + Heuristics", not Strict. A newcomer expects their depth choice to change the explanation style.

(MINOR) "Balanced" table felt high-variance for a newcomer: I busted my full stack in BOTH showdowns I reached (3 all-ins across 2 hands at 100BB). Opponents stack off readily and pots balloon fast; losing two buy-ins in three hands could discourage a new player and made "balanced" feel closer to a maniac table.

(NIT) The winning-hand label "Flush, Queens high" renders in the central pot/community area, near the board and my own hand; at first glance I thought it was labeling MY hand rather than the winner's. A "Bot 5 wins with..." attribution would be clearer.

(NIT) Bot stacks carry over between hands and vary widely (e.g. 13.5 BB vs 383 BB) after I picked a uniform "100 BB" starting stack. Realistic for cash, but unexplained — briefly puzzling for a newcomer.

(NIT) Blind size ($2 BB / 100BB = $200) is never stated on the setup screen, so the initial dollar amounts were unexplained until I either did the math or found the $/BB toggle.

(NIT) At showdown the Mental Math expander reverts to its "Deal a hand and reach the flop..." placeholder right after having shown rich content, which is slightly jarring.
