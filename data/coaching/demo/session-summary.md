# Session summary — demo

Reviewed 4 hands.

## Recurring leaks
- **Playing too many weak hands before the flop** — seen in all 4 hands (9♥4♥ from UTG, Q♥4♦ and
  T♠2♦ off-chart, a thin small-pair call). These off-chart calls are the root of almost every tough
  spot you faced. **What to do:** before calling a raise, ask "would the baseline chart play this?"
  If not, fold — especially out of position.
- **Calling too wide on later streets** — seen 6× (turn/river calls with single-digit equity when you
  needed 24–25%). Once the price is wrong, let the hand go. **What to do:** if you'd need to win
  ~1-in-4 and you're clearly behind, fold.
- **Missing value with big hands** — seen 3× in Hand 3, where you checked a near-lock (~83% → ~94%)
  three streets in a row. **What to do:** when you're this far ahead, bet for value — that's where the
  money is.

## What you're doing well
- When you *did* have a correct price, you called correctly (Hand 2 flop calls ~50%, Hand 3 river call
  ~93%). Your price sense mid-hand is solid.
- You correctly checked/gave up when you had almost no equity (Hand 4 turn/river), avoiding throwing
  good money after bad.

## Try this next session
1. **Fix the entry point:** fold weak, off-chart hands preflop, especially from early position and the
   blinds. Most of your losses trace back to one loose preflop call.
2. **Bet your monsters:** when you flop a set or better, fire for value instead of checking.
